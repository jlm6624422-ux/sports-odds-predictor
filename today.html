Analysis Methodology

  • American odds to implied probability: negative odds: |odds|/(|odds|+100), positive odds: 100/(odds+100)
  • Vig removal: normalize implied probabilities to sum to 100%
  • NBA home court advantage: +3.5% win probability
  • MLB home field advantage: +4.0% win probability
  • Injury adjustment: -1.5% per key player (Out/Doubtful) for affected team
  • ESPN BPI/Predictor blend: 60% ESPN model + 40% record-based model when available
  • MLB model: 70% market odds + 30% (record-based + home advantage)
  • MLB totals: based on combined team win% as proxy for run production
  • Edge calculation: Model Probability - Implied Probability (positive = value bet)
  • Expected total from team pace and offensive/defensive ratings
  • Confidence weighting from data quality and edge magnitude
